WHY THIS DEMO

F · Nested & Reentrant Calls

A cross-rollup call can safely call back across rollups mid-execution — more than once. This demo shows how the protocol verifies that whole nested tree atomically, in a single L1 block, with no live message-passing.

What you should walk away understanding
  • Reentrancy across chains is supported, not just tolerated.
  • Each reentrant hop owns its own sub-array — there's no shared array or counter.
  • Completeness is proven by one rolling hash over the entire nested tree.
