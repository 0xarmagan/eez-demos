WHY THIS DEMO

I · Running the EEZ Devnet with Kurtosis

This is the actual runnable starting point for the Node-operator / Rollup0 track: one Kurtosis command spins up a full disposable EEZ chain — sequencer, composer, proof-signer, and its own private L1 — without touching the real Chiado testnet or a faucet.

What you should walk away understanding
  • Kurtosis's private PoS L1 is disposable and self-contained — no synced Chiado snapshot, no faucet-funded keys, unlike the Docker chiado-up.sh flow.
  • start.sh builds three images and hands off to one `kurtosis run`; the network is then verified against deployments.env, not assumed — scripts/verify-eezl2-deployment.sh checks it live.
  • Host ports are assigned dynamically by Kurtosis — always resolve them through ports.sh rather than hard-coding a port number.
