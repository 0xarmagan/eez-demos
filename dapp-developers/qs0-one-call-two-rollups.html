Counter is the L2 contract. Its increment() function adds one to its stored number and returns it: starting at 0, the result is 1.
CounterAndProxy is the caller contract, separate from the proxy. Its incrementProxy() function saves the result in targetCounter and also increments its own local counter.

ONLY IN THAT BLOCK
Send your transaction to the composer’s RPC, not a plain L1 RPC. In a block where the rollup wasn’t verified, your call reverts ExecutionNotInCurrentBlock. With no matching entry it reverts ExecutionNotFound. Either way the revert comes back through the proxy to your contract. In card 01, the scenario script does the composer’s job.

Each entry, returnData included, is covered by the batch’s proofs. Each rollup sets a minimum proof-system count. Zero removes that minimum, not verification: EEZ still requires a nonempty allowed selection, and every submitted proof must pass. Today’s testnet uses one ECDSA signing key. Card 01’s local run accepts any proof. On L2, Counter runs the same call and finds its entry by the same call hash.
