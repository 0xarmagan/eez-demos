Two Gotchas Before You Build

You have followed a call across and back. Before you write your own contract, check who calls it and when its entry can be consumed. A same-chain test can miss both gotchas.

One gotcha per step, the upstream lines behind it, and a link to the page that covers it in depth.

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
  • 01 · MSG.SENDER IS THE PROXY.Your contract is called by the source's proxy, so an owner check against the source reverts.
  • 02 · AN ENTRY COUNTS ONLY IN ITS BLOCK.A proxy call reverts unless its rollup was verified in the current block.
