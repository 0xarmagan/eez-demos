How to call a contract on another rollup

Calling a contract on another rollup starts as an ordinary encode — a normal call, with no cross-chain-specific ABI.

You send that encoded call to the proxy address, and the proxy's fallback catches everything.

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
  • 01 · ENCODE IT LIKE ANY CALLA normal call, encoded with no cross-chain-specific ABI.
  • 02 · SEND TO THE PROXYIt goes to the proxy address, whose fallback catches everything.
  • 03 · VIEW CALL OR REAL ONE?One self-call probes a transient write — a revert marks it a view call, routed to a read-only lookup.
