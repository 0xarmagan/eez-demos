How to get your contract's address on another rollup

Your contract lives on one rollup. On every other rollup it has a stand-in — a proxy — and anything over there that wants to reach your contract calls the stand-in instead. EEZ routes the call home.

The stand-in's address is derived, not assigned. It comes from your address, your own rollup id, and the manager contract on the far side — so you can compute it before anything is deployed, and reference a counterparty's address before either side exists.

You will rarely create one yourself. The protocol creates the proxy on the first inbound call, so the address is real whether or not you ever call createCrossChainProxy.

One rule: a proxy stands in for a remote address. Asking for one on the manager's own network reverts with SameNetworkProxy.
