WHY THIS DEMO

D · msg.sender Through the Proxy

This is the single most common footgun in cross-chain contract design. msg.sender on the receiving side is the CREATE2 alias, not the caller's real address — so a naive require(msg.sender == owner) passes on the same chain and silently reverts cross-chain.

What you should walk away understanding
  • The destination always sees the alias as msg.sender, never the origin EOA.
  • A same-chain-only access check breaks silently the moment it's called cross-chain.
  • Fix: whitelist the alias itself, derived via computeCrossChainProxyAddress.
