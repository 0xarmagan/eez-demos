WHY THIS DEMO

C · Cross-Chain Call Encoder

For anyone who needs to reproduce what the protocol encodes under the hood — a relayer, an indexer, custom tooling — instead of trusting a black box. This is the exact struct and hash that identify a cross-chain call.

What you should walk away understanding
  • The call target is always the proxy, never the real destination contract directly.
  • The hash's argument order is load-bearing — every on-chain check depends on that exact sequence.
  • Native ETH rides the value field directly, no wrapped-token detour.

two catches
a.
the target is proxy(C), not C
You address the L1 proxy, whose address is derived deterministically via CREATE2 from the target's (rollupId, address). The calldata and value are encoded exactly as for any Ethereum call.
b.
it must be delivered inside a bundle
Call the proxy outside a composer bundle and it reverts — the lookup is not there. That rules out the public L1 mempool. Route it through a composer, a bundle, or account abstraction (see demo B).
