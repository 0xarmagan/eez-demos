WHY THIS DEMO

B · Bundle-Only Proxy Calls

Builders coming from message-passing bridges tend to assume a cross-chain call can be dispatched on its own, live. In EEZ it can't — it only resolves as part of a composer bundle, matched by content hash. This demo draws that line before someone hits a confusing revert.

What you should walk away understanding
  • A proxy call only executes inside a composer bundle — never as a standalone dispatch.
  • Outside a bundle, the identical call reverts with ExecutionNotFound.
  • The authorization guard checks a boolean isProxy flag, not an address.
