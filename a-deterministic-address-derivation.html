WHY THIS DEMO

A · Deterministic Address Derivation

Cross-chain identity in EEZ isn't assigned by a relayer or a bridge — it's pure math. Every (address, origin rollup) pair has exactly one address on every other chain, computable before any proxy is ever deployed. This demo builds that intuition from the CREATE2 math itself.

What you should walk away understanding
  • Your proxy address is derivable off-chain, before deployment — from (rollupId, address) alone.
  • The init-code hash is a shared constant per manager; only the salt changes per proxy.
  • Same account, different alias on every chain — one proxy per (address, chain) pair.
