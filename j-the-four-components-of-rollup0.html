WHY THIS DEMO

J · The Four Components of Rollup0

Every other Rollup0 demo assumes you already know these four pieces. This one is the map: what each of eez-rollup0's four crates actually does, and the order they hand off to each other, end to end — before you look at any single one up close.

What you should walk away understanding
  • The sequencer produces L2 blocks by driving a stock reth node — eez-node wraps reth, it doesn't replace it.
  • The composer posts those blocks to the EEZ contract on L1, but only after the proof-signer — a separate daemon — has independently re-executed and attested them.
  • A deriver can rebuild the identical L2 chain from L1 alone, reading only BatchPosted events — it never talks to the sequencer or the proof-signer.
