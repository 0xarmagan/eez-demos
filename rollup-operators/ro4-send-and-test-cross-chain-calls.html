rbuilder-chiado silently drops the excess beyond ~3 — that's lost tx inclusion, not a queued retry.
Raise this only against a builder proven to include larger bundles atomically. Measure before bumping.
The cap is generous for one op — it's the first thing to check when a wave of txs comes up short.

ROLLUP OPERATORS · 04

How to send and test a cross-chain call

Two transparent proxy fronts sit in front of L1 and L2: sendRawTransaction is held for the next Sync block, while everything else passes straight through.

Driving both fronts is how you read real pass/fail numbers.

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
  • 01 · TWO FRONTS, ONE JOBsendRawTransaction is held for the next Sync block — once per L1 block, for cross-chain work.
  • 02 · MIND THE BUNDLE SIZEEEZ_MAX_USER_TXS_PER_BUNDLE defaults to 3, and rbuilder-chiado silently drops anything past it.
  • 03 · EXERCISE IT FOR REALxchain-test.sh drives both fronts through the full matrix and reports real pipeline metrics.
