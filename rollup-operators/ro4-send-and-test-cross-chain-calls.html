⚠ FRICTION · rbuilder-chiado
rbuilder-chiado silently drops the excess beyond ~3 — that's lost tx inclusion, not a queued retry.
Raise this only against a builder proven to include larger bundles atomically. Measure before bumping.
The cap is generous for one op — it's the first thing to check when a wave of txs comes up short.
