G · Registering a Rollup

The onboarding-flow reference for anyone standing up a new rollup: what joining the EEZ registry actually looks like, end to end.

What you should walk away understanding
  • Registration is permissionless — you deploy your own manager, the registry only records it.
  • rollupId 0 is reserved for Ethereum L1 itself; your first L2 gets id 1.
  • The manager keeps its own id and passes it back explicitly — no reverse lookup needed.
